🔒 Lock Back — How to play

Lock the other team. Free your own team.

Before the round

1Join the Wi-Fi
2Scan the QR code of your game (or open its link)
3Type your 4-digit number (it decides your team) and your name
4Tap Ready
1Type your number and tap Ready
2Round on: coins and locks show up as messages
3A bomb is armed against your team!
4Locked: show your number to be freed
5Round result

⭐ Score points

  • Enemy number → they are locked. +1 point (their team −1)
  • Locked teammate number → you free them. +1 point

⚠️ Watch out

  • An enemy who is already locked can’t be locked again — nothing happens.
  • You can’t enter the same number twice in a row.
  • Your own number does nothing.
  • When you are locked your screen turns red — show your number so a teammate can free you sooner.
  • You can come back only a limited number of times per round (❤️ on your screen). With none left you are out until the next round.

🏆 End of the round

  • The team with more points wins the round.
  • Scores start again at 0 next round.
  • Round wins are counted until the admin resets the game.

🪙 Coins

  • Printed coins are hidden around. Each has its own 4-digit number: type it like a player number.
  • Gold +5 points (wait 30 s) · Silver +3 (20 s) · Bronze +1 (10 s).
  • The wait is per player: you can take the same coin again only after it.
  • Your team’s coins (gold = 5, silver = 3) are its bomb balance.

💣 Bombs

  • Four bombs are hidden too, each with its own number. Type it to arm it against the other team — it costs coins (shown on your screen).
  • After 15 s it explodes and locks some of their players.
  • A bomb against your team? A red alarm shows its picture: find it and type its number. Enough different teammates must type it in time to defuse it (+3).
❓ Tap ? at the top of your screen any time to read these instructions.

🔒 Lock Back — Admin guide

Run up to 3 games from one screen

Open on the laptop: http://localhost:3080/admin

1Open the master board

  • Set the round time at the top (type minutes, or tap 1 / 2 / 3 / 5).
  • Press ▶ Start round on a game. It uses the time you set.
  • The same button becomes ✕ Cancel start during the 3-2-1, then ■ End round while it runs.
  • Each card shows the timer, the score, 🏆 rounds won and how many players are online.

2Bring the players in

  • 📱 Share / QR shows a big QR code and link for that game (http — no browser warning).
  • 🔢 Show numbers (Full board) fills the screen with all numbers: online · offline · free.
  • Players pick a free number from their team. Use Size → Very large if it is hard to read.

3Watch and manage one game (Full board)

  • Scores and 🏆 round wins for both teams.
  • Players: green = online, red = offline. A locked player glows red with a countdown.
  • ± buttons change a team’s points. ✕ removes a player.
  • Activity lists who locked or freed whom.
  • End round / Reset game. Test bots and the “same number twice” switch are for practice only.
  • The Locks and returns box sets how many times each player can come back per round (default 3) and the return time in seconds. With no returns left a player is out (💀) until the next round.

4Coins and bombs

  • On the master board tap 🖨 Print coins, then choose Print all, 🪙 Print coins only (to play without bombs) or 💣 Print bombs only. One coin or bomb per A4 page — hide them in different places.
  • Coin numbers and bomb numbers are 4 digits and never equal a player’s number. They stay the same until you delete the data folder.
  • A bomb costs about 1.2 coins per player of the arming team, needs about 6% of the target team (at least 2 players) to defuse and locks about 10% of the target team. Countdown 15 s, then the bomb rests 30 s.
  • The big screen shows every armed bomb with its timer and a 💥 when it explodes.
